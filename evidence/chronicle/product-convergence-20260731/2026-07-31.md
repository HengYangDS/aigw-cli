# AIGW Product Convergence

## Claim

This record binds the implemented AIGW candidate to one provider-control-plane
contract and records only local candidate observations. It does not claim
hosted CI, publication, installation, or runtime acceptance.

## Observations

- Product authority is limited to Accounts, credentials, Profiles, Routes,
  endpoints, storage policy, and owned client projections.
- External Responses services remain ordinary HTTP endpoints and keep their own
  deployment, traffic, and lifecycle authority.
- Source portability and architecture gates reject personal environment,
  forbidden product coupling, wrappers, aliases, and non-semantic structure.
- The full local common-gate run recorded 47 of 47 passing gates with aggregate
  race coverage of 96.96 percent and zero traceback, warning, failure, HTTP
  error, skip, or missing-tool diagnostic tokens.

## Evidence Boundary

The gate observation above came from the complete staged candidate before this
OpenSpec bootstrap. The bootstrap changes only governance metadata and the
local-state ignore contract; its affected governance gates must be rerun before
this Change is closed. Exact-HEAD ETHOS proof, hosted CI, Forge publication, and
lane retirement remain separate required evidence.
