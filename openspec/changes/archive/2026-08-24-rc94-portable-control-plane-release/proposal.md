## Why

The accepted product now provides portable Token storage, profile-scoped native
Codex providers, truthful deferred authentication, and stricter portable quality
verification. The published and installed product must expose those exact
accepted bytes.

## What Changes

Publish the accepted post-rc.93 product as rc.94 by advancing the version source,
recording the user-visible changes once, and validating the existing release
contract without changing provider, credential, client, projection, or transport
semantics.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

None. Product behavior is owned by the already archived bounded Changes; this
Change owns release identity and chronology only.

## Impact

Only `VERSION`, `CHANGELOG.md`, and this bounded release Change are modified. One
signed product object and annotated tag are projected unchanged to both Forge
peers.
